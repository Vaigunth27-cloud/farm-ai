Problem Statement & Civic Motivation

The Challenge of Modern Urban Governance

Why traditional municipal surveys fail and how AI unlocks real-time digital twins.

Outdated Municipal Registries

Manual cadastre audits take 12-18 months. In Ward 29, official OpenStreetMap registers contained only 62 landmarks (13.3%), leaving 86.7% of utility poles and streetlights completely unrecorded.

Hazardous Cable Burden & Outages

Illegal overhead telecommunication fibers and untagged service drops cause severe structural pole sag, transformer overloads (e.g. ST08 at 4,120.9 kVA/km), and electrical fire hazards with zero digital tracking.

Uncollected Civic Revenue

Commercial structures expand vertically without updated assessments. Lack of OCR hoarding tracking deprives Coimbatore Corporation of commercial property and advertising licensing revenue.
